# Marketplace Buyer-Seller Disclaimer Kit

## 1. Banner copy (persistent, on every listing/product page)

**Short version (for tight UI space):**
> ⚠️ We don't process payments or verify sellers. Meet in person and inspect goods before paying.

**Full version:**
> ⚠️ **Important:** This platform is a listing space only. We are not involved in payments or transactions between buyers and sellers, and we do not verify sellers or products. Please meet the seller in person, inspect the item, and confirm it matches the listing before making any payment.

Style suggestion: yellow/amber background, small warning icon, placed directly above the "Contact Seller" / "Buy Now" button — not buried in a footer.

---

## 2. Safety tips (linked page, referenced from every listing)

- Meet in a safe, public place — daylight hours if possible
- Inspect the item fully before paying
- Never pay in advance for items you haven't seen, especially via bank transfer to an individual
- Bring a friend if meeting a stranger for a high-value item
- If something feels off, walk away — no deal is worth the risk
- Report suspicious sellers using the report button on their listing
